LinFan is installed and the service is running.

IMPORTANT: log out and back in once before starting LinFan.

Access to the daemon is restricted to members of the local group "LinFan Users",
and Windows only applies group membership at sign-in. On a first install your
account was just added to that group, so until you sign out and back in the GUI
reports the service as unreachable even though it is running. In that case the
setup's final page offers a restart -- accepting it covers the sign-out. (On an
upgrade you are already a member and can start right away.)

Start the GUI as a NORMAL USER (Start menu -> "LinFan"), not as administrator;
writing PWM happens in the service, which already runs with SYSTEM rights.

Configuration: %ProgramData%\linfan\config.json (the daemon is the sole writer).
Log:           %ProgramData%\linfan\logs\linfan.log
